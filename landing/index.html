    The problem with most benchmarks

    Most benchmarks pause the world while your model thinks. Reality doesn't.

    In Real-Time Mode the simulator keeps running during planning. Slow models act on stale observations. Drag the planner latency and watch the gripper close on where the cube was.

    Simplified top-down illustration of the dynamic conveyor task (cube at 0.02 m/s). Capture zone shown is illustrative. Measured results are in the benchmarks below.

    How it works

    One owner steps the simulator. The planner pays for every millisecond.

    01MuJoCo engine5-DOF arm, friction gripper, conveyor, seeded failure injector
    02PerceptionOracle or camera, about 1 mm, alpha-beta velocity tracker
    03AgentVerifier V0 · V1 · V2, local Ollama planner, recovery loop
    04Budget auditorVRAM, RAM, watts, temperature, SQLite, Budget Card
    Hold policy: the world advances by the planner's measured latency before the plan is applied

    Budget Cards

    Process vs device VRAM and RAM caps, p95 latency, energy per episode. A missing measurement counts as a failure.

    Failure injection

    Grasp slip, displacement, wrong placement and sensor noise, all seeded and reproducible.

    Three agents

    Open-loop, closed-loop and recovery: detect, diagnose, re-observe, replan.

    Five tasks

    pick_place, smallest, spatial, multi_object and a moving conveyor cube.
